According to Figure 1, how does the BiLSTM branch process the tokenized news text before the final classification head?

GROUND TRUTH ANSWER:
The tokenized and cleaned news text is converted to word embeddings (GloVe, 300-dimensional), passed through the bidirectional LSTM encoder to capture context from both directions, then through an attention layer that weights the most relevant tokens before the final classification head.

This report presents a comparative study of five NLP architectures for classifying news articles into six
categories: Politics, Sports, Technology, Entertainment, Health, and Finance. The dataset contains 18,600
