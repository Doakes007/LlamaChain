5. Discussion
The results confirm transformer-based models significantly outperform traditional approaches for news
classification. The attention mechanism in Figure 5 demonstrates that BERT learns semantically
meaningful token relationships without explicit feature engineering.

RoBERTa achieves 94.1% accuracy with consistent performance across all six categories as shown in
Table 2. Sports category achieves the highest F1 of 0.960 due to distinct vocabulary. Finance has the
lowest F1 of 0.908, likely because financial language overlaps with Politics and Technology categories.

5.2 Limitations
The main limitation is computational cost — RoBERTa requires 4.8x more training time than BiLSTM and

preferable alternatives.
