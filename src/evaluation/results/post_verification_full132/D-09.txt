QUESTION:
What is the purpose of the SynthDoG synthetic document generator?

GROUND TRUTH ANSWER:
SynthDoG is a synthetic document-image generator used to create synthetic training data for Donut's pre-training. It reduces dependence on large-scale real document images and enables easier domain and multilingual extension; the paper shows synthetic data are useful for text-reading pre-training and multilingual settings.

Donut.pdf, pp. 3, 23–24
