NLP-Based News Article Classification

A Comparative Study of Deep Learning Models for Multi-Category Text Classification

Abstract

This report presents a comparative study of five NLP architectures for classifying news articles into six
categories: Politics, Sports, Technology, Entertainment, Health, and Finance. The dataset contains 18,600
articles collected from major news portals. We evaluate Naive Bayes, SVM, BiLSTM, BERT (fine-tuned),
and RoBERTa models. RoBERTa achieves the highest accuracy of 94.1% and Fi score of 93.4%. The
study examines training dynamics, attention mechanisms, and computational trade-offs between
traditional ML and transformer-based approaches.

1. System Architecture

The NLP pipeline consists of six sequential stages as shown in Figure 1. Raw text is first tokenized and
cleaned by removing stop words, punctuation, and applying lowercasing. Word embeddings are generated
using GloVe (300-dimensional) for BILSTM and WordPiece tokenization for BERT/RoBERTa. The BiLSTM
encoder processes sequences bidirectionally to capture context from both directions. An attention layer
weights the most relevant tokens before the final classification head produces probabilities over six
categories.
