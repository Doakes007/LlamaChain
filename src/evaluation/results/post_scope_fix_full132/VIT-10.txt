QUESTION:
How does the amount of pre-training data affect the performance of Vision Transformer models?

GROUND TRUTH ANSWER:
Increasing pre-training data substantially improves ViT performance. With small datasets such as ImageNet, larger ViT models can underperform smaller ones and BiT ResNets. With ImageNet-21k their performance becomes similar, while JFT-300M enables the full benefit of larger ViT models. Figure 4 also shows ViT improving more than ResNets as the JFT subset grows.
