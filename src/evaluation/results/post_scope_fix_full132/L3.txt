QUESTION:
Why does LayoutLMv3 avoid using a pre-trained CNN or Faster R-CNN backbone for extracting visual features?

GROUND TRUTH ANSWER:
It avoids pre-trained CNN or Faster R-CNN backbones because those approaches introduce a heavy computational/parameter bottleneck or require region supervision. LayoutLMv3 instead uses simple linear patch embeddings, which significantly reduces parameters and removes the need for region annotations and complex visual preprocessing.

GROUND TRUTH SOURCE:
LayoutLMv3.pdf, pp. 2–3
