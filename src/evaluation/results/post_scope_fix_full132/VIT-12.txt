QUESTION:
How does the average attention distance change as the depth of the Vision Transformer network increases?

GROUND TRUTH ANSWER:
Average attention distance generally increases with network depth. Some heads attend globally even in low layers, while others remain localized early on; deeper layers increasingly integrate information over larger image regions.

GROUND TRUTH SOURCE:
Vision Transformer.pdf, pp. 8, 20
