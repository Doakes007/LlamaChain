QUESTION:
What are the two types of embeddings that make up the text representation in LayoutLMv3?

GROUND TRUTH ANSWER:
The text representation consists of word embeddings and position embeddings. The position embeddings include 1D sequence-position embeddings and 2D layout-position embeddings based on the text bounding boxes.
