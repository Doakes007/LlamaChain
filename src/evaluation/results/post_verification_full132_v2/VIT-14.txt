QUESTION:
What effect does removing positional embeddings have on ViT performance compared with using positional embeddings?

GROUND TRUTH ANSWER:
Removing positional embeddings causes a large performance drop. In the ViT-B/16 ImageNet 5-shot linear ablation, no positional embedding achieves 0.61382 accuracy versus 0.64206 for 1-D positional embeddings, while the different positional-encoding variants perform similarly to one another.
