QUESTION:
In Figure 6, what examples demonstrate the strengths and limitations of Donut compared with LayoutLMv2 on DocVQA?

GROUND TRUTH ANSWER:
Figure 6 shows that OCR errors can cause LayoutLMv2-based systems to produce incorrect answers: for example, LayoutLMv2-Large-QG outputs an incorrect passenger name and phone number in the shown examples. Donut correctly answers some examples, including the passenger name and phone number, but in the rightmost large-scale image it misses tiny text because of its input-resolution constraint. The paper notes that increasing input resolution can mitigate this limitation.

 In Figure 6, the examples demonstrate the strengths and limitations of Donut compared with LayoutLMv2 on DocVQA as follows:

Strengths of Donut:
1. Donut shows a promising result without OCR, as shown by the high Average Normalized Levenshtein Similarity (ANLS) score on the handwritten documents, which are known to be challenging due to the difficulty of handwriting OCR.
2. Donut shows robust performances, especially in low-resourced situations. It outperformed the LayoutLMv2 accuracy with only 10% of the data, which is only 80 samples, in an extremely low-resourced situation.

Limitations of Donut:
1. OCR errors make a performance upper-bound of the OCR-dependent baselines, such as LayoutLMv2. In the examples provided, Donut incorrectly identified the phone number as 336-723-4100 instead of the correct number 336-723-6100. This error is likely due to OCR errors in the original document.
2. Donut is trained without any supervision for text localization, which may lead to incorrect attention to text regions in some cases. This is evident in the examples where Donut fails to correctly identify the phone number.

Overall, Donut shows promising results in handling handwritten documents and low-resourced situations, but its performance is limited by the quality of the OCR and its ability to accurately localize text in images.
