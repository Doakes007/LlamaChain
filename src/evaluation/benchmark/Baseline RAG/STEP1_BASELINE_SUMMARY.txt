IMPORTANT INTERPRETATION
------------------------
This step is descriptive baseline analysis. It does not yet claim that the verification
framework improves performance. It establishes the observed performance of the frozen
RAG outputs before the verification experiment.

The source dataset contains 54 rows with no recorded failure_type. Those are retained as
"No recorded failure" for analysis; this does NOT mean they are proven failure-free.

The question_type field contains many paper-specific labels. They are preserved rather
than silently collapsing them into broad categories. Broad modality/category analysis
should be performed only after defining an explicit, reproducible mapping.
